Case Study: Social Media Content Strategy for Ultimate Pro

Social Media Strategy  /  Oct 2023 – Jan 2024  /  Instagram, X and LinkedIn

The client

Ultimate Pro sells accounting and ERP software to businesses, in four packages: Services Pro, Easy Pro, Alshamel Pro and Alshamel ERP, plus a newer cloud version, Alshamel Web. The audience is business owners and finance teams in Saudi Arabia.

What the content had to do

Accounting software is a dry topic, and each package serves a different size of company. The content needed to explain the differences between the packages clearly, while teaching the audience enough about finance and compliance to trust the brand.

What I delivered

Four monthly content plans, from October 2023 to January 2024, written in Arabic. The January 2024 plan alone contains:

  • 12 posts: the text inside each design, a caption, and the platforms to publish on
  • 4 reels: on-screen text and captions
  • 4 stories: each one pointing back to a related post

How the plan was built

  • Education first. Posts on VAT, the difference between review and audit, cost centres, the principles of a good accounting system, and how to spot fraud.
  • One post per package. Each of the four packages got its own promotional post, written for the type of company it suits.
  • Short formats for reach. Reels on data security tips, comparing the four packages, the new cloud packages and the sales and invoicing system.
  • Stories that reuse the posts. One story per package links back to its post.
