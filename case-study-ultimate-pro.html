Social Media Content Strategy for Ultimate Pro

Software (Accounting / ERP)  /  Oct 2023 – Jan 2024  /  Instagram, X and LinkedIn  /  Written in Arabic

The client

Ultimate Pro sells accounting and ERP software to businesses in four packages: Services Pro, Easy Pro, Alshamel Pro and Alshamel ERP, plus a newer cloud version, Alshamel Web. The audience is business owners and finance teams in Saudi Arabia.

The challenge

Accounting software is a dry topic, and each package suits a different size of company. The content had to explain the differences between the packages clearly, while teaching the audience enough about finance and compliance to trust the brand.

What I did

  • Wrote four monthly content plans, October 2023 to January 2024, in Arabic. The January 2024 plan alone has 12 posts, 4 reels and 4 stories, each with the text inside the design, a caption and the platforms to publish on.
  • Education first: posts on VAT, the difference between review and audit, cost centres, the principles of a good accounting system, and how to spot fraud.
  • One promotional post per package, written for the type of company it suits.
  • Short formats for reach: reels on data-security tips, a comparison of the four packages, the new cloud packages and the sales and invoicing system.
  • Stories that reuse the posts: one story per package points back to its post.
