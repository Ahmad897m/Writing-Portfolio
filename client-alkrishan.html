Social Media Content Strategy for Alkrishan (الكريشان)

E-commerce (Natural Hair & Body Oils)  /  Jun – Jul 2024  /  Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat  /  Written in Arabic

The client

Alkrishan is an online store for natural hair and body oils.

The challenge

An online shop with many similar products has to teach customers which oil does what, and show how to use it. The content had to turn product features into practical routines.

What I did

  • Wrote the July 2024 plan: 12 posts, 6 reels and 8 stories, with captions and on-screen text. Chose music for each reel.
  • Wrote one educational post, reel or story per oil (castor, almond, bitter almond, coconut, glycerin, black seed, wheat germ and more), explaining the benefit.
  • Wrote how-to content that shows the steps for using hair oils and body oils.
  • Added engagement posts that ask followers to pick their favourite oil or test their knowledge.

A sample from the plan

لماذا نستخدم المواد الطبيعية في الزيوت؟

  1. آمنة وفعّالة: خالية من المواد الكيميائية الضارة.
  2. تغذية عميقة: غنية بالفيتامينات والبروتينات.
  3. صديقة للبيئة: تحافظ على كوكبنا.
  4. روائح منعشة: طبيعية وخفيفة.

الكابشن: اختاري زيوت الكريشان الطبيعية لجمال صحي ومستدام!

What I delivered

1 full monthly plan (12 posts, 6 reels, 8 stories), Jun – Jul 2024.
