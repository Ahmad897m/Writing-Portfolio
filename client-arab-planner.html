Social Media Content Strategy for Arab Planner

Events & Ramadan Decorations  /  Jan – Mar 2024 (incl. Ramadan 2024)  /  Instagram  /  Written in English

The client

Arab Planner is an events company that also offers decorative installations, such as the lantern, moon and crescent designs used in Ramadan gatherings.

The challenge

Ramadan is a short, competitive season for event planners. The company needed a campaign that announced its Ramadan gathering service, showed its decorations and pushed bookings before the season ended.

What I did

  • Wrote the Ramadan 2024 campaign: a service announcement, product posts for the Lantern, Moon and Crescent designs with a 10% Ramadan offer, and a thank-you and booking post.
  • Wrote a scene-by-scene reel script from the opening scene to the booking call to action.
  • Wrote two further monthly plans, January and March 2024.

A sample from the plan

Special Ramadan Offer: 10% Off — Ramadan Lantern Design

Available in 3.3 meters and 1.2 meters.

Lightweight metal structure resembling an Arabian lantern.

Caption: Enjoy a warm and magical atmosphere during the fasting month with our captivating 3D lantern lights.
