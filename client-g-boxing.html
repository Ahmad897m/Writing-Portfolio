Social Media Content Strategy for G Boxing

Fitness (Boxing Club)  /  Oct – Nov 2023  /  Instagram  /  Written in English and Arabic

The client

G Boxing is a boxing and fitness club in Bahrain.

The challenge

The club was new to the market, and many people think boxing classes are only for boxers. The content had to widen that view and reach three groups at once: adults, parents of young people and women.

What I did

  • Wrote two monthly plans in English with an Arabic version of every caption. The November 2023 plan has 11 posts and 2 stories.
  • Announced the monthly class schedule and the club’s availability on a booking app, and introduced a new coach.
  • Wrote separate messages for youth classes (confidence, focus, discipline), weight-loss classes and ladies-only classes.

A sample from the plan
