Social Media Content Strategy for GME (Gulf Middle East)

Industrial Machinery & Spare Parts  /  Sep 2023 – Feb 2024  /  Instagram  /  Written in English

The client

GME supplies industrial machinery, spare parts and motor rewinding services in the Gulf and the wider Middle East and North Africa.

The challenge

A technical B2B supplier has to look credible to engineers and buyers rather than casual followers. The posts had to explain what GME does and how it works, in plain English.

What I did

  • Wrote 5 monthly content plans from September 2023 to February 2024, in English, with hashtags for each post.
  • Covered the company’s vision and mission, its working process step by step, and practical maintenance tips.

What I delivered

5 monthly content plans, Sep 2023 – Feb 2024.
