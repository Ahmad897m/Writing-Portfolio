Social Media Content Strategy for Nazrat Mwtawer (نظرة المطور)

Construction & Insulation  /  Nov 2023 – Apr 2024  /  Instagram  /  Written in Arabic

The client

Nazrat Mwtawer is a contracting company in Saudi Arabia, based in Riyadh with a branch in Jeddah. It specialises in foam, thermal and water insulation for buildings.

The challenge

Insulation is a service people only look for once something goes wrong. The content had to explain the three insulation types, correct common misconceptions and make a technical company feel trustworthy.

What I did

  • Wrote 3 monthly plans from November 2023 to April 2024. The November 2023 plan has 13 posts and 6 reels.
  • Wrote explainer posts: open-cell versus closed-cell foam, the types of thermal insulation materials, and the steps of waterproofing.
  • Wrote myth-versus-fact posts on foam insulation and the environment.
  • Wrote benefit-list reels and brief 3D animation directions for the rainy-season and full-insulation reels.
