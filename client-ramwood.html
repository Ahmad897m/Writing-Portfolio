Social Media Content Strategy for Ramwood

Home Furniture & Décor  /  Sep 2023 – Jul 2024  /  Instagram  /  Written in Arabic

The client

Ramwood is a home furniture and décor brand.

The challenge

Furniture is bought on looks and trust. The Instagram page needed a steady monthly flow of content that mixed useful decorating advice, product promotions and customer proof, so that it did not read as one long advertisement.

What I did

  • Wrote 11 monthly content plans from September 2023 to July 2024, including a bundles campaign.
  • Each plan has a dated publishing schedule with posts, 4 reels and 4 stories, and for every item the text inside the design and a caption.
  • Mixed tips posts (how to choose and style pieces), promotional posts and posts built around Google reviews.

What I delivered

11 monthly content plans, Sep 2023 – Jul 2024.
