Social Media Content Strategy for Sports World (سبورتس وورلد)

Sports Equipment Supplier  /  Dec 2023 – Mar 2024  /  Instagram  /  Written in Arabic

The client

Sports World supplies gym and fitness equipment to clubs and to people building a home gym, and distributes the Align Pilates brand.

The challenge

The buyers are gym owners and home-gym enthusiasts, and equipment is bought after seeing it in action. The content had to show products working, and speak to the business case for owners as well as the fitness goals of individuals.

What I did

  • Wrote a dated plan with 6 posts, 10 reels and 4 stories, plus further monthly plans.
  • Wrote a caption and an on-screen line for every reel, with direction on which footage and which reference reel to follow.
  • Wrote two audiences into the plan: posts and stories for club owners (“your profits grow with our equipment”) and reels for individuals and home gyms.
  • Added educational posts such as the importance of warming up before exercise.

A sample from the plan

لا تنس الإحماء قبل ممارسة أي نشاط رياضي، فهو ضروري لأنه:

  1. يزيد درجة حرارة الجسم ويعد العضلات للتمرين
  2. يزيد مرونة العضلات ومدى حركتها
  3. يزيد معدل ضخ الدم والأكسجين للعضلات
  4. يحمي العضلات من إصابات خطيرة

الكابشن: لا تهمل الإحماء قبل ممارسة رياضتك المفضلة، فهو ضروري لتجنب الإصابات وتحقيق أقصى استفادة من التمرين!
