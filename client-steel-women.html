Social Media Content Strategy for Steel Women (ستييل وومن)

Fitness (Women-only Gym)  /  Dec 2023 – Apr 2024  /  Instagram  /  Written in Arabic (conversational dialect)

The client

Steel Women is a women-only gym that offers CrossFit classes, personal training and a nutrition package.

The challenge

The gym needed to sell memberships at the end of the year and to build a relationship with women who were not yet members. The tone had to feel like a conversation with a friend and a trainer, not an advertisement.

What I did

  • Wrote the December 2023 plan: 8 posts, 1 reel and 2 stories. Two more plans followed, up to April 2024.
  • Wrote the captions in a conversational dialect, addressed to women.
  • Wrote advice posts (nutrition, back pain after weight training, abs), a “term you did not know” post, and posts on personal training and CrossFit classes.
  • Designed the year-end discount campaign: a post that lays out the membership tiers, plus a countdown story sequence. Wrote a nutrition-package reel that sells the service through useful advice.
